The account menu lists the other Omni accounts signed in on your browser, so switching to one takes a single click. When more than one account is signed in, Sign out ends only the account you use in Backfeed, and a new Sign out of all accounts entry ends every one. Links to your Omni account now open it as the same account you use in Backfeed
